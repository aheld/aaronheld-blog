I grew up on cholent, the Eastern European stew that simmers beans into a single soft mass for the better part of a day. I loved it. My wife does not, and she's not a curry person either. That's been the real obstacle to eating more plant-based at our house, until now.

## Notes for stage 4

- Hook 3 is probably strongest for LinkedIn specifically, it's recipe-content-forward and the algorithm/audience there responds better to concrete value up front than to a personal anecdote lead-in.
- Hook 1 is the better Bluesky/Threads opener if we want a shorter, punchier personal angle, those platforms tolerate more personality-first posts.
- Not part of a series. Standalone, personal-category post, a genuine departure from the blog's usual professional/leadership content, so the LinkedIn post should probably say that explicitly ("something different today") rather than blend in like it's another engineering post.
- Every platform post should link to Nora Cooks' original prominently, not just the blog post, per the credit/contrarian resolution from research.
