# Sources — High-Protein "Marry Me" Beans, Peas & Mushrooms

## Primary source (the recipe this is derived from)

**"Marry Me Chickpeas"**
- Author: Nora Taylor, Nora Cooks (noracooks.com)
- URL: https://www.noracooks.com/marry-me-chickpeas/
- What it is: a vegan, one-pan take on the viral "Marry Me Chicken" trend — chickpeas in a sun-dried tomato and cashew/cream sauce, ready in ~15 minutes.
- Why credited as primary: this post's recipe is a direct derivative. Every structural beat (garlic + aromatics, sun-dried-tomato-style base, chickpeas, cream, spinach, basil finish) descends from Nora's original. Nothing here would exist without her recipe.

Original ingredient list and method (for reference/comparison, not for republishing verbatim):
- 1 tbsp olive oil, 5 cloves garlic, 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, 1/2 tsp oregano, 1/2 tsp red chili flakes, 1 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 cans chickpeas, 1 cup vegetable broth, 1 tbsp tomato paste, 1 cup vegan cream
- 2 cups baby spinach, 4-5 basil leaves, 1/2 cup vegan parmesan (optional)
- Method: bloom garlic in oil, add sun-dried tomatoes + spices, add chickpeas/broth/tomato paste/cream/spinach, simmer 5 min, finish with basil + parmesan.

## Aaron's development process (primary personal source)

Aaron worked through the adaptation in a series of prompts to Gemini, iterating live while cooking. Captured in a shared chat log (screenshotted/PDF'd by Aaron since the live share link requires Google sign-in and can't be crawled). Key decision points from that transcript:

1. **Sun-dried tomatoes were the first thing to go** — not on hand. Gemini suggested tomato paste + a pinch of sugar as the closest flavor match, or seared mushrooms for a non-tomato umami swap. Aaron picked mushrooms.
2. **Protein was the explicit goal** — Aaron asked directly "how can I add protein to this." Options surfaced: soy milk instead of cream, blended white beans, stir-in tofu/seitan, edamame or peas, hemp hearts.
3. **Cashew cream + blended white beans** became the base — homemade cashew cream (soaked cashews blended smooth) with half a can of cannellini beans blended directly in. This one move does double duty: it thickens the sauce without cornstarch *and* adds protein invisibly.
4. **Peas went in because Aaron's wife likes them** — a direct, undisguised personal reason, not a nutrition-optimization one. Worth keeping in the post verbatim — it's the most human beat in the whole log.
5. **Bean swap**: Aaron asked about replacing one can of chickpeas with red lentils vs. cannellini beans. Verdict from the exchange: red lentils would dissolve into a dhal-like porridge and lose the chunky texture; cannellini beans keep the classic look/feel. Aaron went with one can chickpeas + one can cannellini beans.
6. **Mushrooms formalized**: final recipe sears 8oz cremini/button mushrooms first, building a browned base before the garlic goes in — replacing the sun-dried tomatoes' umami role entirely.
7. **Macros**: final estimate ~330 kcal and ~17g protein per serving (6 servings), versus roughly ~10g protein/serving in Nora's original chickpea-only version.

## Final recipe (as cooked, per the Gemini transcript)

**High-Protein "Marry Me" Beans, Peas & Mushrooms** — prep 10 min, cook 15 min, serves 6

*Cream base*: 3/4 cup raw cashews (soaked in boiling water 5 min, drained), 1/2 can (~3/4 cup) cannellini or navy beans (rinsed), 1 1/4 cups fresh water — blended smooth.

*Skillet*: 1 tbsp olive oil, 8 oz cremini or button mushrooms (sliced), 5 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tbsp tomato paste, 1/2 tsp oregano, 1/2 tsp red chili flakes, 1 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper, 1 (15oz) can chickpeas, 1 (15oz) can cannellini beans, 1 cup frozen peas, 1 cup vegetable broth, 2 cups baby spinach (sliced), 4-5 basil leaves (chopped).

*Method*: sear mushrooms until browned → add garlic → add tomato paste + spices, cook 1 min → add chickpeas, cannellini, peas, broth, blended cream, simmer ~5 min until thickened → stir in spinach until wilted → finish with basil off heat.

*Macros per serving (1/6 recipe)*: ~330 kcal, 17g protein, 11.5g fat, 42g carb (10.5g fiber), ~680mg sodium.
